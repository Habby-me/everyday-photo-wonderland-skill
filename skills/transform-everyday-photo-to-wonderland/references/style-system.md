# Style system

Use this system to offer selectable creative directions from observable visual traits. Do not depend on an artist name or a bundled third-party image.

## Shared visual DNA

- Reframe an ordinary object, food, plant, machine, or place as an inhabitable landscape.
- Create a strong size contrast between one giant anchor and tiny people, animals, or object-creatures.
- Use friendly hand-drawn contours, simplified faces, tactile paint or paper texture, and a calm storybook mood.
- Favor botanical greens, cream, pale aqua, ochre, orange, or coral accents.
- Show small acts of work, play, rest, cooking, harvesting, exploring, or caring.
- Keep the world inviting and gently surreal rather than epic, glossy, cinematic, or frightening.

## Five routes

### A. 森系绘本水彩

Best for garden, travel, street, family, children, and pet photos with a strong central object.

- Medium: delicate ink outline, transparent watercolor wash, visible paper grain.
- Composition: lush layered foliage; one giant object fills roughly 45–70% of the frame; miniature figures interact through ladders, paths, pools, or platforms.
- Palette: sage, leaf green, pale mint, warm soil brown, small orange/red accents.
- Feeling: gentle adventure, nostalgic picture book, quiet summer day.
- Tradeoff: dense foliage can weaken face likeness or hide source-scene details.

### B. 萌宠蔬果童话

Best for pets, food, picnics, farms, markets, birthdays, and playful family scenes.

- Medium: soft watercolor or colored-pencil texture with rounded shapes and friendly outlines.
- Composition: oversized produce or food becomes a table, boat, house, field, or harvest site.
- Characters: small rabbits, mice, blob creatures, or simplified human avatars.
- Palette: fresh pea green, lime, cream, peach, terracotta, with occasional sky blue or lavender.
- Feeling: cute, edible, cozy, busy but safe.
- Tradeoff: animal avatars reduce literal identity unless clothing, pose, and signature features are retained.

### C. 白底编辑插画

Best for group portraits, dining, gifts, celebrations, products, or images that need a clean social-post layout.

- Medium: crisp hand-drawn editorial illustration with dry-brush or colored-pencil texture.
- Composition: isolated giant object on cream/white negative space; characters orbit, sit inside, or work around it.
- Palette: dark green, yellow-green, pumpkin orange, soft pink, black accents.
- Feeling: clever, graphic, warm, easy to read at small size.
- Tradeoff: removes most environmental context and feels less immersive.

### D. 复古平涂蜡笔

Best for indoor snapshots, games, tables, architecture, nostalgic memories, and photos with blocky geometry.

- Medium: matte gouache, crayon or screen-print grain, chunky shapes, slightly imperfect edges.
- Composition: crop in close; turn repeated objects into walls, furniture, rooms, or a maze.
- Palette: limited olive, forest green, cream, mustard, orange, and muted coral.
- Feeling: retro children's editorial, warm and graphic.
- Tradeoff: form simplification is high; facial and material detail may be reduced.

### E. 现代绿调线稿异想

Best for city, technology, indoor objects, vehicles, machines, bottles, aquariums, and futuristic scenes.

- Medium: clean digital contour lines, flat color, minimal shading, precise graphic shapes.
- Composition: an appliance, container, machine, or object becomes a complete ecosystem or vertical world.
- Palette: emerald, teal, chartreuse, pale lime, with a small yellow/orange accent.
- Feeling: modern, surreal, playful science-fiction.
- Tradeoff: monochrome green can overpower skin tones and personal color cues.

## Selection matrix

| Source-photo strength | Prefer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Plants, garden, outdoor landmark | A | Natural fit for layered botanical scale cues |
| Pet, food, picnic, childlike play | B | Supports cute avatars and tactile produce worlds |
| Group, meal, product, clean post | C | Preserves readable silhouettes and negative space |
| Interior, game, table, repeated geometry | D | Repeated shapes become architecture |
| Machine, bottle, bathroom, city, tech | E | Containers and devices become ecosystems |

## High-impact controls

Offer these as simple choices only when unresolved:

1. **Identity fidelity** — high likeness / recognizable cartoon / symbolic avatar.
2. **World transformation** — light stylization / one giant anchor / fully inhabited micro-world.
3. **Character mode** — illustrated humans / animal avatars / object-creatures.
4. **Medium** — watercolor ink / colored pencil / matte gouache-crayon / clean digital line art.
5. **Density** — airy / balanced / lush.
6. **Palette** — source-photo colors / botanical green with warm accent / green monochrome / custom.

For portraits, default identity fidelity to high. For objects or food, default world transformation to one giant anchor. For crowded photos, default density to balanced and simplify the background.
